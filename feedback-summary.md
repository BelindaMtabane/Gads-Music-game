## 1. Event context

On **11 June 2026 at 6pm**, we presented **Rhythm Raiders** at a **playtesting session hosted by the Goethe-Institut**. Attendees played a laptop build, watched the start flow and main chase level, and gave written/oral feedback on **AI-assisted dialogue**, **UI**, **narration**, **sound**, and **overall feel**.

**Purpose:** Gather external critique from players outside our class (mixed ages) to refine Part 2 project for Part 3 submission.

**Evidence:** Photo and raw notes — see `attendance-evidence.md`.

## 5. Recurring themes (3+ mentions)

1. **UI must improve** — all five players (fonts, menus, layout).  
2. **AI/narrator dialogue is understandable** — four to five players; helps onboarding.  
3. **More music** — P2, P3; aligns with Rhythm Raiders identity.  
4. **Artifacts harder to spot** — P3, P4 (gameplay clarity).  
5. **Positive core idea** — music + chase + gaming vibe is working.
